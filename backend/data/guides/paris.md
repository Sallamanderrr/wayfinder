# Paris

## Overview
Paris is divided into 20 arrondissements spiralling out from the Louvre. The Seine splits the city into the Right Bank (the Louvre, the Marais, Montmartre) and the Left Bank (Saint-Germain, the Latin Quarter, the Eiffel Tower). The metro is dense, so the city is easy to cover in a few days if you group sights by area.

## Best time to visit and climate
- April–June and September–October are pleasant (highs 16–24 °C), with long evenings in early summer.
- July and August are warm (highs around 25 °C, with occasional heatwaves above 35 °C); some small restaurants close in August.
- Rain is spread through the year. It is rarely heavy, but showers are frequent, so an umbrella is useful in any season.
- Winter is grey and cool (highs 7–9 °C) but good for museums, and December brings festive lights.

## Neighbourhoods to stay in
- Le Marais (3rd–4th arrondissements): historic mansions, galleries and cafés; central and lively.
- Saint-Germain-des-Prés (6th): classic Left Bank cafés and boutiques; pricier.
- Latin Quarter (5th): student energy, bookshops and good value.
- Montmartre (18th): a village feel and views over the city; hilly and further from the centre.
- Canal Saint-Martin (10th): trendy and local, near Gare du Nord.

## Getting around
- From Charles de Gaulle airport (CDG): the RER B train reaches Gare du Nord and Châtelet in about 35–50 minutes; the airport ticket costs about €14. The Roissybus to Opéra takes 60–75 minutes. Taxis charge fixed fares of about €56 to the Right Bank and €65 to the Left Bank.
- From Orly airport (ORY): metro line 14 runs directly into the centre in about 25 minutes.
- In the city, a single metro/RER ticket costs about €2.50. Load tickets on a Navigo Easy card or a phone. The Navigo weekly pass (Monday to Sunday) is the best value for longer stays.
- The Vélib' bike share and walks along the Seine are great in good weather.

## Typical costs
Approximate prices in 2026:
- Espresso at the counter: €2–3. Croissant: €1.30–1.80.
- Bistro lunch "formule": €18–25.
- Dinner at a mid-range restaurant: €35–55 per person.
- Mid-range hotel: €170–260 per night.
- City tax: a few euros per person per night, added by the hotel.

## Food and drink
Croissants and pains au chocolat from a proper boulangerie, steak-frites, duck confit, French onion soup, cheese plates and natural-wine bars. Service is included in menu prices ("service compris"); leaving a few euros for good service is a nice gesture, not an obligation.

## Practical tips
- The Louvre is closed on Tuesdays and the Musée d'Orsay on Mondays. Book timed tickets for both, and for the Eiffel Tower summit.
- The Paris Museum Pass (2, 4 or 6 days) covers more than 50 museums and monuments. It pays off if you visit three or more paid sights per day.
- Notre-Dame reopened in December 2024. Entry is free, and you can reserve a time slot to skip the queue.
- Beware of petition and "found gold ring" scams around the major sights.

## Day trips
- Versailles: the palace and gardens, about 45 minutes away by RER C. The palace is closed on Mondays.
- Giverny: Monet's house and garden (open April to October), by train to Vernon plus a shuttle bus.
